What this prototype does

This dashboard provides a structured framework for organizations to plan information governance before connecting AI assistants to enterprise document repositories. It supports visual mapping of source status, document ownership, lifecycle separation (active vs. archive), and intended retrieval boundaries to highlight areas needing management or specialist review.

What this prototype does not do

This tool does not provide legal advice, technical AI auditing, cybersecurity assessment, privacy impact assessment, records-retention determinations, or compliance certification. It does not scan networks, evaluate actual permissions, test AI models, or guarantee accurate retrieval or data security. Data entered is fictional or redacted for demonstration only.

Why source status matters

An AI assistant connected to an uncontrolled information environment may retrieve content that appears relevant but has unclear currency, authority, ownership, approval status, access suitability, or business purpose. This prototype helps organize review questions; it does not determine source authority, legal status, or technical system behavior.

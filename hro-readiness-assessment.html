GCG — AI IMPLEMENTATION READINESS

Who is qualified to act on this AI output — and how would you know?

A frontline operation adopting AI carries the same risk shape as a flight deck: one unqualified action can turn a small error into a large failure. This assessment maps whether your AI-adoption plan has a named, qualified human behind every point where AI output could drive a real decision — informed by high-reliability-organizing (HRO) practice and the deference-to-expertise principle.

Measurement Discipline

AI adoption, time saved, user satisfaction, licenses issued, prompts, and training attendance are not automatically financial ROI. Financial results require organization and appropriate finance review of benefit realization and full lifecycle cost.

What this prototype does

  • Organizes AI use-case evidence and baselines.
  • Structures value hypotheses and lifecycle cost inputs.
  • Tracks operational results and human-review controls.
  • Supports management measurement and documentation.

What this prototype does not do

  • Does not provide legal advice, compliance determinations, or safety engineering.
  • Does not provide tax, investment, valuation, accounting, or financial assurance.
  • Does not evaluate employees, score individuals, or make employment decisions.
  • Does not perform technical AI audits, psychometrics, or model validation.

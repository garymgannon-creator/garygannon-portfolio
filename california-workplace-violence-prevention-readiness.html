  • Confirm training completion and recordkeeping approach.
  • Confirm incident-log, review, and corrective-action workflow.

Suggested Implementation Roadmap

Days 1–30

  • Confirm responsible program owner and alternate.
  • Organize current written materials and evidence.
  • Clarify reporting routes.
  • Create training and incident-log documentation plan.
  • Address priority warehouse follow-up.

Days 31–60

  • Implement employee involvement/consultation record.
  • Conduct and document workplace-violence-specific hazard review process.
  • Finalize training records and refresher calendar.
  • Confirm emergency response documentation and appropriate specialist questions.

Days 61–90

  • Review action completion with leadership.
  • Establish periodic review calendar.
  • Maintain evidence register.
  • Determine whether a broader implementation or multi-site support engagement is appropriate.

The Documented Methodology

1
Root-cause research — understanding the actual WCIRB formula inputs driving EMR, not just the outcome.
2
Proactive Loss History monitoring — ordering updated Loss History monthly/quarterly for relevant policy years, projecting the upcoming EMR before renewal.
3
Active reserve management — directly questioning open claims and reserve amounts with the carrier where they appear unsupported.
4
Return-to-Work Program — structured pathway back to productive roles.
5
First-Aid Policy — handling minor injuries at the appropriate clinical level.
6
Modified-Duty Plan — defined structure for assigning injured employees to modified roles.
7
Occupational health provider coordination — briefing the treating physician on real modified-duty options available.
8
Direct injured-worker communication — reducing confusion that leads to attorney involvement.
9
Retaliation-avoidance discipline — consistent with California Labor Code §132a, no adverse action for filing a claim.

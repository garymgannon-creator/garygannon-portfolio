How to Use This Prototype

  1. Start with employer fact intake.
  2. Organize work areas, operations, shifts, workforce groups, employer interfaces, tenant/vendor interfaces, contractor interfaces, and work-location-control facts.
  3. Map documentation and evidence for management review.
  4. Track employee-participation and workforce-communication evidence.
  5. Track training-support evidence for employees and supervisors.
  6. Track records-request workflow dates and custodians.
  7. Record operational changes that may require management review.
  8. Assign internal actions and specialist-review flags.
  9. Separate current statutory readiness controls, official guidance references, and draft/future-state monitoring.
  10. Route legal, HR, safety, security, facilities, privacy, health-care, workers’ compensation, contract, reporting, and threat-management questions to qualified client professionals.
  11. Obtain qualified attorney review after GCG services are used.
  12. Keep data fictional/redacted unless a separate approved client data-governance process exists.
  13. Do not treat dashboard status labels as legal conclusions.

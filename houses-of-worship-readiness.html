GCG — HOUSES OF WORSHIP SAFETY READINESS

A free, honest look at your congregation's safety readiness — six areas, no jargon.

This self-assessment follows the same framework the U.S. Department of Homeland Security recommends for houses of worship. Answer honestly; there's no wrong answer, only a clearer picture of where to focus next.

Sources used to build this: CISA, Mitigating Attacks on Houses of Worship Security Guide (Dec. 2020) and Protecting Places of Worship: Six Steps; U.S. Secret Service National Threat Assessment Center, Mass Attacks in Public Spaces research; FBI Active Shooter Attack Prevention and Preparedness guidance; American College of Surgeons/Hartford Consensus, Stop the Bleed. A Free 5-Minute Skill Worth Having Regardless

Emergency responders often cannot reach an active scene until it's secured — a real, documented delay. Stop the Bleed (American College of Surgeons/Hartford Consensus, completely free) teaches bystanders to control severe bleeding in minutes, including improvising a tourniquet from a belt or bag strap. Not affiliated with this tool — a genuinely separate, worthwhile resource.

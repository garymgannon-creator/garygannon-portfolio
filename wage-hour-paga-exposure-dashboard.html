1 · Meal & rest break compliance check

Brinker v. Superior Court (2012) sets the meal-break timing rule; Augustus v. ABM Security Services (2016) requires rest breaks to be fully duty-free — an employer cannot keep a guard on-call, radio-on, or reachable during the break. Enter a shift length to see what's required.

Meal breaks: 30-min unpaid before end of hour 5 (shifts >5 hrs); second 30-min break before end of hour 10 (shifts >10 hrs), waivable by mutual consent if total shift ≤12 hrs and first break wasn't waived. Rest breaks: 10-min paid per 4 hrs worked "or major fraction thereof." Augustus v. ABM Security Services, Inc., 2 Cal.5th 257 (2016).

2 · Missed-break premium pay calculator

Labor Code §226.7 requires one additional hour of pay at the employee's regular rate for each workday a compliant meal break isn't provided, and a separate hour for each workday a compliant rest break isn't provided — capped at two premium hours per employee per workday regardless of how many individual breaks were missed that day.

3 · Exempt / non-exempt classification screener

California uses a two-part test: a salary basis test and a duties test. Meeting the salary alone never establishes exempt status — job title and salary don't override actual job duties.

2026 general threshold: $70,304/yr ($5,858.67/mo)
—

2026 CA figures: general exempt threshold $70,304/yr (2x the $16.90/hr state minimum wage, 40-hr week); computer professional $58.85/hr / $122,573.13/yr; licensed physician/surgeon $107.17/hr. Only guaranteed base salary counts toward the threshold — bonuses and commissions do not. Higher local or industry-specific minimum wages (fast food, health care) will raise the effective threshold further and are not reflected in this screener.

4 · PAGA penalty exposure estimator

The 2024 PAGA reform (AB 2288 / SB 92, effective for claims filed or noticed on or after June 19, 2024) replaced the old $100-initial/$200-subsequent structure. The default penalty is now $100 per aggrieved employee per pay period, with a $200 tier reserved for specific aggravating circumstances, and new caps for employers who took proactive compliance steps.

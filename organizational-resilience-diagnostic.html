GCG — ORGANIZATIONAL RESILIENCE DIAGNOSTIC

Most resilience gaps are discovered during the disruption, not before it.

This self-assessment maps your organization against four categories of disruption — financial shock, operational continuity, legal exposure, and technological change — using the same categories a real 29-year, no-structural-advantage operation had to survive in practice.

Note on the reference point: the illustrative case behind this tool's design is one operator's documented 29-year record of continuous revenue growth through the 2008 recession, COVID-19, ongoing litigation, and repeated technological disruption. This is presented as one real, evidenced experience — not a universal template every organization can expect to replicate, and not a guarantee of any outcome.

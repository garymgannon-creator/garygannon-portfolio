How to Use This Prototype

This dashboard is a front-end demonstration of the AI-Enabled Evidence Portfolio and Market-Validation Sprint methodology.

It demonstrates a structured approach to mapping self-reported capability evidence (Part 1) to hypothetical documented market problems (Part 2), leading to the design of interactive proof-of-work artifacts (Part 3).

A separate Organizational Pathway supports mapping workflows and defining safe administrative AI-assistive uses, strictly separated from any individual employment decision.

Data Privacy: All data is fictional. The interface is completely self-contained in your browser. No data is stored externally.
